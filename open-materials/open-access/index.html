**Welcome!** These slides are intended to provide learners with an **introduction to the current academic publishing system**, the different **types of open access**, the lifecycle of **open research papers** and what *everyone* can contribute to more transparent research.

### Who are these materials for?

- **Educators** in higher education with or without familiarity with open research planning to **teach open research novices**
- **Self-learners** with no or limited familiarity with open research planning to **develop and build** up on their open research skills

### Prerequisites

<div style="font-size: 0.8em;">
| Prerequisite   |  Description  | Link/Where to find it   |
|------------|------------|------------|
| UNESCO Recommendations on Open Science | For a first overview of Open Research, particularly pp 6-19 | [Download here](https://unesdoc.unesco.org/ark:/48223/pf0000379949) |
</div>

### Learning objectives

At the end of this session, you or your learners will be able to ...

- **Understand** the publishing system (and how publishers make $$$$)
- **Scrutinize** different Open Access policies and types, and **assess** their implications for both authors and readers
- **Explain** the process of publishing an original manuscript as a preprint
- **(Re-)Evaluate** the purpose of preprints and your *own role* in the academic publishing world
